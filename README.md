# SAP Technical Solution Design — Case Study

A Technical Solution Design exercise completed as the hands-on project for the **SAP Technology Consultant Professional Certificate** (SAP University Alliances Program). [Verify certificate](https://coursera.org/verify/professional-cert/3ZE0O3HVZEDM)

The exercise is based on a fictional case study company, **RenewAgra**.

## 📌 Context

RenewAgra is a fictional agricultural company used as the certification's case study, made up of three business units:

- **CropCo** — crop production and processing (low SAP maturity, largely Excel/paper-based)
- **TransCrop** — logistics and fleet management (legacy 1980s SCM system)
- **EnvoData** — data analytics and environmental monitoring (already on SAP S/4HANA, highest maturity)

The exercise involved evaluating the current landscape of each business unit, proposing SAP products to close capability gaps, and identifying gaps to close before go-live.

## 🎯 Why I'm sharing this

This exercise let me practice the core Fit-to-Standard thinking a consultant applies: reading a customer's current landscape, matching it against SAP's product portfolio, and being explicit about gaps and open questions rather than glossing over them.

## 📌 Note

RenewAgra is a fictional company and dataset used for certification purposes, not a real client engagement. It's shared here to demonstrate how I approach solution design, not as a record of professional consulting experience.
